01 / ABOUT US

MORE THAN
AN ASSOCIATION.

JEKO MEMBERS is an association of Physical Education students who share the same passion for sports, teamwork and education.

From the classroom to the field, every experience becomes part of our journey together.

We are not just preparing ourselves to become athletes, but future educators who will inspire the next generation.

02 / OUR PURPOSE

VISION & MISSION

What drives us forward, together.

🎯

OUR VISION

To become a leading association that shapes disciplined, passionate and future-ready Physical Education students who inspire excellence both on and off the field.

🚀

OUR MISSION

To build a strong, united community through teamwork and leadership, to organize meaningful programs and activities, and to nurture every member's potential through discipline, welfare and continuous growth.
