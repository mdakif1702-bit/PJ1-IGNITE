About us

MORE THAN
A SPORTS CLUB.

JEKO is the official sports club of IPG Kampus Kota Bharu, uniting students who share the same passion for sports, teamwork and education.

From training to competition, every experience becomes part of our journey together.

We are not just preparing ourselves to become athletes, but future educators who will inspire the next generation.

🏅

Est. by the students of IPG Kampus Kota Bharu, built on discipline, welfare and teamwork.

Our purpose

VISION & MISSION

What drives us forward, together.

🎯

OUR VISION

To become a leading sports club that shapes disciplined, passionate and future-ready students who inspire excellence both on and off the field.

🚀

OUR MISSION

To build a strong, united community through teamwork and leadership, to organize meaningful programs and activities, and to nurture every member's potential through discipline, welfare and continuous growth.
